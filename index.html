ABOUT

WEB VTEC is a digital agency built to help businesses of every kind — from niche service providers like beauty professionals and home service businesses to larger commercial brands — establish, automate, and dominate their online presence. The platform offers custom built websites, AI-powered calling systems, AI chatbots on websites, AI hiring screeners, promotional design, and tailored digital solutions for a wide range of industries. WEB VTEC was founded by Carlanvi Roberts, who developed the platform in 2025. Self-taught in digital development, he now uses modern technologies to create solutions that deliver real, lasting value to the businesses that use them. His main goal is to help more businesses get online, automate their operations, and build a strong digital presence through WEB VTEC — giving even small local operations the tools to compete and grow on an international level. Carlanvi is also a professional welder, certified through the HEART Trust training programme.
