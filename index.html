ABOUT

WEB VTEC is a digital agency built to help businesses of every kind — from niche service providers like beauty professionals and home service businesses to larger commercial brands — establish, automate, and dominate their online presence. Every website, AI calling system, AI chatbot, AI hiring screener, and platform WEB VTEC delivers is hand-coded from scratch — there are no drag-and-drop builders or templated no-code tools involved. WEB VTEC was founded by Carlanvi Roberts, who wrote and developed the platform himself in 2025. Self-taught as a full-stack developer, he codes every project line by line in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and builds custom AI-driven systems tailored to each business, so clients get real, purpose-built technology rather than a generic template with their logo on it. His main goal is to help more businesses get online, automate their operations, and build a strong digital presence through WEB VTEC — giving even small local operations the tools to compete and grow on an international level. Carlanvi is also a professional welder, certified through the HEART Trust training programme.
